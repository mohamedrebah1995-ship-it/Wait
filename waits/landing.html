How it works

Two taps while you wait. That's it.

No forms, no fuss. Logging a wait takes about as long as reading this sentence — and it helps every driver near you.

1

Arrive at the restaurant

Tap Arrived when you pull up. A timer starts on its own — no typing, no setup.

2

Log your wait

The moment you've got the food, tap Got it. Your real wait time is saved in a single tap.

3

See the community data

Every driver nearby instantly sees the live and typical wait at each restaurant — so nobody walks into a 30-minute queue blind.

Install in 10 seconds

MOdelivr runs right in your browser — no app store, no download. Add it to your home screen so it opens like a normal app.

📱 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dots menu, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Add.
